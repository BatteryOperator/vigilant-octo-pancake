Privacy Policy

Last Updated: September 2026

Our Commitment to Privacy

At Blub Blub Studios LLC, we are committed to protecting the privacy of children and families. This Privacy Policy describes data handling in the Story Glider app and information you choose to send to support.

Story Glider App Data

Story Glider does not require a child account. The app does not send reading progress, story activity, or child profile information to Blub Blub Studios. It contains no advertisements or third-party analytics or tracking SDKs.

Purchases and Apple Services

Apple processes in-app purchases and restores through the App Store. Story Glider uses Apple's verified transaction information to determine whether the story library is unlocked. Apple handles payment information under its own privacy practices. Blub Blub Studios does not receive your payment card details through the app.

Reading Progress and Preferences

Reading progress and preferences are saved locally on the device. Story Glider does not provide its own cloud sync service. Device backups are controlled by your Apple device and account settings. You can clear reading progress in the app's parent Settings.

Support and Website Links

If you email support, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address and issue description. Please avoid sending personal information about a child. Opening a website link leaves the app and connects to that website.

Children's Privacy

Story Glider is designed for early readers. No child account, name, email address, microphone recording, or location is requested by the app. Purchase paths and links to support and policies are in parent-facing areas.

Your Rights

Reading progress is controlled on your device. For questions about information you have sent to support, contact us using the address below.

Contact Us

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page and updating the "Last Updated" date. Material changes will also be described through the App Store as appropriate. Any change that involves new data collection would require user consent.
