Common questions

What age group is Story Glider designed for?

Story Glider is designed for emerging readers in kindergarten through second grade who are practicing phonics and developing their reading skills.

What is included, and is there a subscription?

You can begin with the included free stories. A one-time in-app purchase unlocks every story in the included 20-level library. There is no subscription.

Does Story Glider require an internet connection?

The included stories and narration work offline after installing the app. An internet connection is needed to purchase, restore purchases, and open support or policy pages.

Does the app collect any data?

No child account is required. Reading progress is saved on the device, and the app has no ads or third-party analytics. Apple handles purchases and restores. See our Privacy Policy for more details.

How do I restore my purchase on a new device?

To restore the library unlock, sign in to the Apple Account used for the purchase and connect to the internet. In Story Glider, open a locked story, complete the parent gate, and tap Restore Previous Purchase on the purchase screen.

What phonics approach does Story Glider use?

Story Glider uses a systematic, sequential phonics approach, progressing through 20 levels from simple to more complex sound patterns. The stories provide reading practice; the app does not assess spoken reading or guarantee learning outcomes.

If something isn’t working

Start here

Try these troubleshooting steps:

  1. Restart the app completely (force quit and reopen)
  2. Ensure your device is running the latest iOS version
  3. Check that you have the latest version of the app from the App Store
  4. Restart your device
  5. If the issue continues, contact support before deleting the app. Reading progress is stored locally and may be lost if the app is removed.

Tell us what happened

Please contact us with details about the problem you're experiencing, including:

  • Device model and iOS version
  • App version
  • Description of the issue
  • Steps to reproduce the problem

Please avoid including personal information about a child.
